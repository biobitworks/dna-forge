# Citation list — endometriosis, PCOS, sex-specific evidence gaps and downstream AI

**Reviewed 2026-11-14.** This document and [TSV](CITATION_REGISTRY_20261114.tsv) / [JSON](CITATION_REGISTRY_20261114.json) registry give each source a stable SRC identifier. Source verification, rights to underlying data, and evidence of execution are separately recorded. None of these external studies has been admitted into the official Biobase competition model.

## Clinical and molecular literature

1. **SRC09 — Self-report symptom-based endometriosis prediction using machine learning** (2023). [Article, PMC10073113](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10073113/). Survey recruited women through Facebook groups, with an author-reported test AUC 0.94; respondent age and ethnicity were not recorded. Self-selected case/control recruitment creates transportability concerns. This is NOT our Kaggle AUC or an independently reproduced result.
2. **SRC10 — Bane et al., GSE153739: transcriptional profiling of mid-proliferative eutopic endometrium** (2021). [GEO accession](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE153739), BioProject PRJNA643835. Bulk RNA sequencing, **4 endometriosis and 3 control tissue samples**. Small discovery dataset, not a large clinical diagnostic validation.
3. **SRC11 — Duempelmann et al., GSE266265: endometriosis endometrial single-cell atlas** (public 2026). [GEO accession](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE266265), BioProject PRJNA1106473. **35 endometriosis + 25 control donors**, about **466,371 cells**. The processed atlas is roughly 38.9 GB. Cells belong to donors and must never be split across train and test as independent patients.
4. **SRC22 — Duempelmann et al., Endometriosis-related alterations in the endometrium revealed by integrated single-cell and AI-powered approaches** (Nature Communications, 2026). [DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-73020-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73020-4). Peer-reviewed companion to GSE266265, not another independent patient cohort.
5. **SRC13 — Hassan MH, Reiter E, Razzaq M, Automatic ovarian follicle detection using object detection models** (Scientific Reports 14, 31856, 2024). [DOI:10.1038/s41598-024-82904-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-82904-8). RetinaNet and YOLO on **mouse ovary histology**; reported test mAP 0.83 versus 0.75. **Not human PCOS training data.** Original images are available from corresponding author; manuscript figures have CC BY-NC-ND restrictions; code: Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14192196.
6. **SRC14 — Pathology Outlines: polycystic ovary topic.** [Reference page](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/ovarypco.html). Access blocked by robots. This is a proposed pathologist educational resource, **not a verified annotation set or clinical diagnostic standard**.
7. **SRC15 — Barad T, Endometriosis Etiology: Hypothesis of Maternal Microchimerism** (Preprints.org, 2021). [Preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202109.0372). Explicitly **not peer-reviewed**. Etiology is PROPOSED, not demonstrated.
8. **SRC16 — Lack of Evidence That Male Fetal Microchimerism is Present in Endometriosis** (2018). [Article, PMC5933168](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5933168/). FISH study examined 31 patients, 19 cases and 12 controls, and reported **no Y chromosome signals** in the sampled tissues. NEGATIVE for detectable male fetal microchimerism under the method, **not** a test refuting maternal microchimerism.
9. **SRC17 — Rei C, Williams T, Feloney M, Endometriosis in a Man as a Rare Source of Abdominal Pain: A Case Report and Review of the Literature** (2018). [DOI:10.1155/2018/2083121](https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/2083121). Single histologically/IHC-confirmed ectopic endometrial-like tissue case involving the vas deferens. Does **not** establish male prevalence, a universal hormonal mechanism, or participant self-described cisgender identity.
10. **SRC18 — Bazelmans et al., Immunohistochemical Evaluation of Potential Biomarkers for Targeted Intraoperative Fluorescence Imaging in Endometriosis** (2024). [Article, PMC11611954](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11611954/). 84 tissue specimens including endometriosis and adjacent nonlesion tissue; a 10-antibody panel highlighted **MMP11** in glandular/stromal tissue and **VCAN** in stroma. **IHC findings are not pixel masks or available model labels.**
11. **SRC19 — Miller JE et al., T helper 17 axis and endometrial macrophage disruption in menstrual effluent provides potential insights into the pathogenesis of endometriosis** (F&S Science 3, 279–287, 2022). [DOI:10.1016/j.xfss.2022.04.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xfss.2022.04.007). 14 surgically confirmed cases and 19 controls; flow cytometry and transcriptional/cytokine immune associations.
12. **SRC20 — Wilson TR et al., Analysis of menstrual effluent uncovers endometriosis-specific cell populations and impaired cellular pathway processes** (bioRxiv, 2025). [DOI:10.1101/2025.08.21.671582](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.08.21.671582). **Preprint** reporting altered macrophage/neutrophil states and clearance pathways. No causal macrophage invasion claim established.
13. **SRC21 — Single-cell analysis identifies distinct macrophage phenotypes associated with prodisease and proresolving functions in the endometriotic niche** (PNAS, 2024). [Article, PMC11420174](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11420174/). Supports investigating immune-cell states and tissue compartments, without conferring access to annotated training images.

## Rules for evidence promotion

**Source citation ≠ dataset permission ≠ local download ≠ histologist-annotated labels ≠ model training ≠ independently observed accuracy.** Article licenses do not necessarily extend to supplementary datasets, images, assay files, patient records or software weights. Keep source URL and DOI identity distinct from local bytes, FCO occurrence and computational receipts. All clinical and bioassay sources above remain **NOT_ADMITTED** to the frozen Biobase model.

Most studies report categories such as men/women, menstrual status, anatomy or hormone exposure, **not verified cisgender identities**. Do not infer cis/trans identity, chromosomes, sex at birth, fertility, or immune status from titles. Collect only the minimum separately defined and consented variables relevant to each research question. Missing identity data should remain UNKNOWN, not guessed.

The registry TSV checksum is actually computed. It identifies **our authored reference manifest**, not web page authenticity, legal permission or a cryptographically signed model root.
